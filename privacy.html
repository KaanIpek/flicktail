Flicktail — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Flicktail has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics, and we never see your data. The game itself stores everything on your device. The one exception is advertising, which is served by Google AdMob — see below.

What the game stores

Flicktail saves your progress on your own device, using your browser's local storage (on the web) or the app's local storage (on iOS). That save contains:

  • which stops you have finished and how many stars you earned
  • your best scores in Endless, Rush Hour, The Shift and the Daily
  • your daily streak and the drinks you have discovered
  • which drink skin you own and which one you are wearing
  • your sound and aim-assist settings

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to read it. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data) deletes it permanently.

What the game does not do

  • No account, e-mail address, name or password is ever requested.
  • We collect no location, contacts, photos or files.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included.
  • We do not sell your data. We have no server and receive nothing from you.

Network use

The game downloads its own pictures and music the first time you play, then works entirely offline. It makes no other network requests.

Advertising

Flicktail shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner displayed inside the bar scene while you are playing a round, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch when a round runs out of drinks. Nothing is shown on menus, and the rewarded video is never forced.

To select and measure those ads, Google may collect the device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and basic device information. That data goes to Google, not to us; we only ever see aggregate earnings. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob documentation.

On iOS, personalised advertising requires your explicit permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline — or never see the prompt — the ads you get are non-personalised, and no advertising identifier is used to build a profile of you. You can change the choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Flicktail is not directed at children. Because it depicts cocktails it is rated 12+ on the App Store, and its ad requests are not marked as child-directed.
