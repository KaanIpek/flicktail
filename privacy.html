Flicktail — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Flicktail does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What the game stores

Flicktail saves your progress on your own device, using your browser's local storage (on the web) or the app's local storage (on iOS). That save contains:

  • which stops you have finished and how many stars you earned
  • your best scores in Endless, Rush Hour, The Shift and the Daily
  • your daily streak and the drinks you have discovered
  • which drink skin you own and which one you are wearing
  • your sound and aim-assist settings

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to read it. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data) deletes it permanently.

What the game does not do

  • No account, e-mail address, name or password is ever requested.
  • No advertising identifier, device identifier or location is collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK is included.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Network use

The game downloads its own pictures and music the first time you play, then works entirely offline. It makes no other network requests.

Advertising

Flicktail currently contains no advertising. If a rewarded video ad is ever added — to offer extra drinks after a failed round — this policy will be updated before it ships, and the ad provider's own data practices will be named here.

Children

Flicktail is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13. It is not directed at children as defined by COPPA, but it would be compliant either way, because there is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
