How to play

Flicking
Pull back anywhere on the table and let go, like a slingshot. The drink goes the opposite way to your pull, and further the harder you pull.
Merging
Two of the same drink that touch become the next one up. Chain several in one shot for a combo bonus.
Stars
Finishing the level's drink earns one star. The bar under your score shows what the next star costs.
Running out
The cooler is finite in the campaign. Drinks that park too close to your launch line clog the bar and end the round, so keep the near end clear.

Modes

World Tour and country tours
180 stops across 21 countries. Every country ends on its own signature drink, which is added to your collection when the tour is complete.
Rush Hour
Unlimited flicks, but the bar keeps sending drinks and speeds up. Let the pile park past the striped line and the shift is over.
The Shift
Twelve tickets, one cooler. Mix what each ticket asks for and roll it onto the dock. Every flick you did not need pays a bonus.
Daily and Endless
The Daily gives everyone the same board and keeps a streak. Endless runs until the bar clogs.

Common questions

I lost my progress
Progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the app or clearing site data removes it. There is no cloud save yet.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After the first play everything is cached and the game runs with no connection.
Can I turn the music off?
Settings on the title screen has separate switches for music and effects, plus an aim-line toggle.
How do I get a drink skin?
Skins are earned with stars from the Skins screen. Nothing in the game costs money today.
